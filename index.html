01 / Biography

Designing clarity
with energy.

A multidisciplinary designer connecting business goals, brand personality and visual craft across static, motion and emerging digital experiences.

I’m Aman Sinha, a Graphic & Packaging Designer and Video Producer based in Noida. I translate brand stories into visual systems that feel clear, memorable and ready to perform.

My experience spans Luxor Writing Instruments, Agatsa, Inshorts, Sportskeeda and Xpert—moving between packaging, D2C creatives, social campaigns, editorial graphics, motion design and video production.

Alongside design practice, I’m pursuing an MBA in Digital Transformation at BITS Pilani. That combination helps me think beyond the individual asset: how creative work supports customer experience, communication and growth.

I’m also expanding into UI/UX and 3D, with a focus on strong fundamentals, practical tools and ideas that can move across media.
